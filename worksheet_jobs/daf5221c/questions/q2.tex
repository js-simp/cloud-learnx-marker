\noindent The diagram shows the cross-section of a satellite dish, modelled as a circle with centre $O$. $PR$ is a diameter of the circle, and $Q$ is a point on the circumference. $PQ$ and $QR$ are chords forming a triangle inscribed in the circle.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=2]
    \coordinate (O) at (0,0);
    \coordinate (P) at (180:1);
    \coordinate (R) at (0:1);
    \coordinate (Q) at (125:1);

    \draw (O) circle (1);
    \fill[black] (O) circle (0.7pt);

    \draw[thick] (P) -- (R);
    \draw[thick] (P) -- (Q);
    \draw[thick] (Q) -- (R);

    \node[left] at (P) {$P$};
    \node[right] at (R) {$R$};
    \node[above left] at (Q) {$Q$};
    \node[below] at (O) {$O$};

    \pic [draw, angle radius=6mm, "$35^\circ$"] {angle = R--P--Q};
    \pic [draw, angle radius=8mm, "$y$"] {angle = P--R--Q};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\noindent Angle $QPR = 35^\circ$.

\noindent Work out the size of angle $y$.
