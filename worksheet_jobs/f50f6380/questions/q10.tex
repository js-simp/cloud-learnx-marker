\begin{question}
\noindent A History classroom is having a rectangular reading bench built for its Space Exploration and Ancient Civilisations book displays.

The bench has length $(3x + 2y)$ metres and width $(x + y)$ metres, as shown in the diagram below.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale = 1]
    \def\lengthA{6}
    \def\heightA{3}

    \coordinate (A) at (0,0);
    \draw (A) -- ++(0:\lengthA) coordinate(B) -- ++(90:\heightA) coordinate (C) -- ++(180:\lengthA) coordinate[pos=0.5] (M) coordinate (D) -- cycle coordinate[pos=0.5] (N);

    \node at (M) [anchor = south] {$3x + 2y$};
    \node at (N) [anchor = east] {$x + y$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\noindent The perimeter of the bench is 22 metres.

\noindent The length is also 2 metres more than three times the width.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item[(a)] Show that $2x + \dfrac{3}{2}y = 5.5$, and write down a second equation connecting $x$ and $y$ using the second piece of information.
    \vspace{3cm}
    \answermarks{2}

    \item[(b)] Solve your equations simultaneously to find the values of $x$ and $y$.
    \vspace{4cm}
    \answerplain{2}
\end{enumerate}
\end{question}
