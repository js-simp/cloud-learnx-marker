\begin{question}
\noindent A historian is organising a rectangular archive room to store ancient manuscripts and novels collected during a space exploration museum exhibit.

The length of the room, in metres, can be written as $(x + 7)$ and the width as $(x - 4)$.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item[(a)] Show that the area of the room, in $\text{m}^2$, can be written as $x^2 + 3x - 28$.
    \vspace{3.5cm}
    \answermarks{2}

    \item[(b)] The area of a second, smaller display room is given by the expression
    \[ x^2 - 5x - 24 \]
    Factorise this expression fully.
    \vspace{3.5cm}
    \answerplain{2}
\end{enumerate}
\end{question}
