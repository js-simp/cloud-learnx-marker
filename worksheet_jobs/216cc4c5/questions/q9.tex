\begin{question}
\noindent An archaeology society is designing two rectangular excavation plots, $A$ and $B$, on the site of an ancient observatory once used to track the movement of the stars.

Plot $A$ has length $(3x + 2y)$ metres and width $(2x + 3y + 3)$ metres.

Plot $B$ has length $(x + 4y)$ metres and width $(2x + 2y + 5)$ metres.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=1]
    % Plot A
    \draw[thick] (0,0) rectangle (5,3);
    \node at (2.5,-0.5) {$3x + 2y$};
    \node at (-1.3,1.5) {$2x + 3y + 3$};
    \node at (2.5,3.6) {Plot $A$};

    % Plot B
    \draw[thick] (8,0) rectangle (11.5,3.6);
    \node at (9.75,-0.5) {$x + 4y$};
    \node at (12.7,1.8) {$2x + 2y + 5$};
    \node at (9.75,4.2) {Plot $B$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\noindent The perimeter of Plot $A$ is equal to the perimeter of Plot $B$.

\noindent The society also knows that the width of Plot $A$ is $3$ metres more than the length of Plot $A$.

\noindent Show that this information leads to the two equations
\[
x - y = 3 \qquad \text{and} \qquad 2y - x = 5,
\]
and hence find the value of $x$ and the value of $y$.

\end{question}
