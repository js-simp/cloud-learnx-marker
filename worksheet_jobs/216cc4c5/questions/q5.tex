\begin{question}
\noindent A history student is organising her collection of novels and space exploration books on a shelf.

The number of books she owns, $x$, satisfies the equation:
\[
\frac{2(x - 5)}{3} + 4 = x - 9
\]

Solve the equation to find the value of $x$.
\vspace{5cm}
\answereq{x}{3}
\end{question}
