\begin{question}
\noindent A rectangular room in a museum is used to display a scale model of the Solar System, based on a diagram found in an old astronomy book that a student was reading for their History of Science project.

The rectangular floor has a length of $(3x + 2)$ metres and a width of $(x - 1)$ metres, as shown below.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=1]
    \def\lengthA{6}
    \def\heightA{3}

    \coordinate (A) at (0,0);
    \draw (A) -- ++(0:\lengthA) coordinate (B) -- ++(90:\heightA) coordinate (C) -- ++(180:\lengthA) coordinate[pos=0.5] (M) coordinate (D) -- cycle coordinate[pos=0.5] (N);

    \node at (M) [anchor = north] {$3x + 2$};
    \node at (N) [anchor = east] {$x - 1$};
    \node[anchor=west] at ($(B)!0.5!(C)$) {$x - 1$};
    \node[anchor=south] at ($(D)!0.5!(C)$) {$3x + 2$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

Find, in terms of $x$, an expression for the perimeter of the room.
Give your answer in its simplest form.
\vspace{4cm}
\begin{flushright}
    \answerplain{3}
\end{flushright}
\end{question}
