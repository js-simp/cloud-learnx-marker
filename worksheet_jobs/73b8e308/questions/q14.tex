\begin{question}
\noindent $A$, $B$ and $C$ are points on a circle, centre $O$. $TC$ is a tangent to the circle at $C$.

\noindent $AC$ passes through $O$, so $AC$ is a diameter of the circle.

\noindent Angle $ABC = 180^\circ - 2x$ is not given directly; instead, the angle $BAC = 34^\circ$ is marked at $A$, and $TC$ is the tangent at $C$.
