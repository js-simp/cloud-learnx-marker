\begin{question}
\noindent A ground control station, $G$, tracks a satellite as it passes overhead. At the moment of closest approach, the satellite's ground-track position, $S$, is on a bearing of $074^\circ$ from $G$, at a distance of $340$ km.

The satellite then continues along its ground track in a straight line to point $T$, on a bearing of $164^\circ$ from $S$, a further distance of $210$ km.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.8, transform shape]
    \coordinate (G) at (0,0);
    \coordinate (S) at (3.263, 9.397);
    \coordinate (T) at (8.075, 7.376);

    % North lines (dashed)
    \draw[dashed] (G) -- (0,4);
    \draw[dashed] (S) -- (3.263, 13.397);

    % Main path
    \draw[thick, ->] (G) -- (S);
    \draw[thick, ->] (S) -- (T);

    % Angle arcs
    \pic [draw, angle radius=8mm, "$74^\circ$"] {angle = (0,4)--(G)--(S)};
    \pic [draw, angle radius=8mm, "$164^\circ$"] {angle = (S)--(3.263,13.397)--(T)};

    % Labels
    \node at (G) [below left] {$G$};
    \node at (S) [above] {$S$};
    \node at (T) [right] {$T$};
    \node at (0,4.3) [left] {N};
    \node at (3.263,13.7) [left] {N};

    \node at (1.5, 5) [left] {$340$ km};
    \node at (5.7, 8.7) [above] {$210$ km};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

Ground control needs to send a repositioning signal directly from $T$ back to $G$.

Work out the bearing of $G$ from $T$.

\vspace{5cm}
\answerequnit{}{$^\circ$}{4}
\end{question}
