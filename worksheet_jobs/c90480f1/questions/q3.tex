\begin{question}
\noindent A space agency's engineering lab has a diagonal support strut that braces a satellite testing platform against a wall.

\noindent The strut makes an angle of $37^\circ$ with the horizontal floor. The foot of the strut is fixed to the floor at a horizontal distance of $2.4$ m from the wall.

\noindent Calculate the length of the support strut.

\vspace{4cm}

\begin{flushright}
    \answerunit{m}{3}
\end{flushright}
\end{question}
