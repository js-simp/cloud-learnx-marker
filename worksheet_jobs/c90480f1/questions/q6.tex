\begin{question}
\noindent A historian is analysing the trajectory data of an early satellite launch. As part of the calculation, she needs to solve the equation
\[
\sin \theta = 0.629
\]
for an angle $\theta$ that lies between $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$.

\noindent Find \textbf{both} possible values of $\theta$ in this range, giving each answer correct to 1 decimal place.

\answerlines{2}{3}
\end{flushright}
\end{question}
