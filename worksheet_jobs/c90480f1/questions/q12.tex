\begin{question}
\noindent Three remote research stations, $P$, $Q$ and $R$, are used to track the trajectory of a high-altitude balloon launched as part of a stratospheric observation programme.

Station $Q$ is 14.8 km from station $P$. Station $R$ is 21.3 km from station $Q$.

The angle between the lines of sight $PQ$ and $QR$, measured at station $Q$, is $A = 137^\circ$.

Calculate the distance $PR$ between stations $P$ and $R$, giving your answer to 3 significant figures.
