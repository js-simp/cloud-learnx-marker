\begin{question}
\noindent Two mathematically similar water containers are used to mix paint for a Model United Nations art installation designed by the school's UI/UX and Digital Art society.

\noindent The volume of the smaller container is $(3x + 6)$ cm$^3$.\\
The volume of the larger container is $(24x + 3)$ cm$^3$.

\noindent The ratio of the volume of the smaller container to the volume of the larger container is $1 : 9$.

\noindent Given that the two containers are similar, find the value of $x$.

\vspace{5cm}

\begin{flushright}
    \answereq{x}{4}
\end{flushright}
\end{question}
