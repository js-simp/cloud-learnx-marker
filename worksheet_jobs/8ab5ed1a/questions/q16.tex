\begin{question}
\noindent An environmental scientist is tracking the migration of a flock of geese. She sets up two observation stations, $A$ and $B$, on a flat coastal plain, where $B$ is $18$ km from $A$ on a bearing of $072^\circ$.

At a certain moment, a tagged goose is spotted at point $G$. From station $A$, the bearing of $G$ is $128^\circ$. From station $B$, the bearing of $G$ is $200^\circ$.

Find the distance $BG$, giving your answer to 3 significant figures.

\vspace{0.3cm}
\noindent \textit{(Hint: work out the interior angles of triangle $ABG$ first, using bearings and back-bearings.)}

\vspace{7cm}

\begin{flushright}
    \answerunit{km}{5}
\end{flushright}
\end{question}
