\begin{question}
\noindent As part of a Model United Nations exhibition, a diagonal support strut is fitted to hold up a large display screen. The strut, the vertical screen and the horizontal base form a right-angled triangle, as shown in the diagram.

The screen stands vertically and is $2.4$ m tall. The foot of the strut is fixed to the ground $1.0$ m away from the base of the screen. The strut connects the top of the screen to this point on the ground.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=1, transform shape]
    \coordinate (A) at (0,0);
    \coordinate (B) at (3,0);
    \coordinate (C) at (0,4);

    \draw[thick] (A) -- (B) -- (C) -- cycle;

    \draw pic[draw, angle radius=2.5mm] {right angle=C--A--B};

    \node at (A) [below left,font=\small] {Base of screen};
    \node at (B) [below right,font=\small] {Foot of strut};
    \node at (C) [above left,font=\small] {Top of screen};

    \draw (A) -- (B) node[midway, below] {$1.0$ m};
    \draw (A) -- (C) node[midway, left] {$2.4$ m};
    \draw (B) -- (C) node[midway, above right] {strut};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\noindent Calculate the length of the strut.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.

\end{flushright}
\end{question}
