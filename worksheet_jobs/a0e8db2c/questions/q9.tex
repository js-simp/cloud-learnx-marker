\begin{question}
\noindent A UI/UX design team is timing how quickly a new app screen loads for users testing a prototype.

They use the formula for average speed of data transfer:
\[
v = \frac{d}{t}
\]
where $v$ is the average speed (in megabytes per second, MB/s), $d$ is the amount of data transferred (in megabytes), and $t$ is the time taken (in seconds).

During one test, a badminton club's booking app transfers $d = 45$ megabytes of data in $t = 7.5$ seconds.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item[(a)] Work out the average transfer speed, $v$, during this test.
    \vspace{3cm}
    \answerequnit{v}{\text{MB/s}}{2}

    \item[(b)] In a second test, the app must transfer 60 megabytes of data. For a smooth user experience, the average transfer speed must be at least 8 MB/s.

    Work out the maximum time, in seconds, that this transfer can take.
    \vspace{3.5cm}
    \answerequnit{t}{\text{s}}{1}
\end{enumerate}
\end{question}
