\begin{question}
\noindent A planetarium designs two spherical models of a newly discovered exoplanet and its host star, which are similar in shape (both perfect spheres) and to scale with each other.

\noindent The small model, representing the exoplanet, has a radius of $4$ cm.\\
The large model, representing the host star, has a radius of $10$ cm.

\noindent The volume of the small model (exoplanet) is $268$ cm$^3$ (to 3 s.f.).

\begin{enumerate}
    \item[(a)] Calculate the volume scale factor between the small model and the large model.
    \vspace{3.5cm}
    \answerplain{2}

    \item[(b)] Calculate the volume of the large model (host star), giving your answer to 3 significant figures.
    \vspace{4cm}
    \answerunit{$\text{cm}^3$}{2}
\end{enumerate}
\end{question}
