\begin{question}
\noindent A scientific supply company manufactures calibration weights in the shape of solid metal spheres. All spheres of a given weight set are geometrically similar and made from the same metal, so they have the same density throughout the range.

\noindent Weight $P$ is a small sphere of radius $2.4$ cm and has a mass of $87$ grams.\\
Weight $Q$ is a larger sphere, similar to $P$, with radius $6$ cm.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item[(a)] Show that the linear scale factor from $P$ to $Q$ is $2.5$.
    \vspace{2.5cm}
    \answermarks{1}

    \item[(b)] Work out the volume scale factor from $P$ to $Q$.
    \vspace{2.5cm}
    \answerplain{2}

    \item[(c)] Hence work out the mass of weight $Q$, giving your answer to 3 significant figures.
    \vspace{4cm}
    \answerunit{g}{2}
\end{enumerate}
\end{question}
