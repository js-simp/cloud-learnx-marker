\begin{question}
\noindent A UI/UX designer is creating a circular logo badge for a Model United Nations conference. The badge is modelled as a circle with centre $O$, where $AC$ is a diameter. A decorative triangle $ABC$ is drawn inside the circle, with point $B$ on the circumference. To make the design symmetric, the designer ensures that $AB = BC$.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=2.4]

    \coordinate (O) at (0,0);
    \draw[name path=circ] (O) circle (1);

    \coordinate (A) at (180:1);
    \coordinate (C) at (0:1);
    \coordinate (B) at (90:1);

    \fill[black] (O) circle (0.8pt);

    \draw[thick] (A) -- (C);
    \draw[thick] (A) -- (B);
    \draw[thick] (B) -- (C);

    \node[left] at (A) {$A$};
    \node[above] at (B) {$B$};
    \node[right] at (C) {$C$};
    \node[below] at (O) {$O$};

    \pic [draw, angle eccentricity=1.5, angle radius=0.6cm, "$x$"] {angle = C--A--B};

\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\noindent Given that $AB = BC$, show that $x = 45$.\\
Give reasons for each stage of your working.
