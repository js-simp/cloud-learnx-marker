\noindent In the diagram below, $TA$ is a tangent to the circle at $A$, and $OAT$ is a triangle where $O$ is the centre of the circle. $OB$ is a radius drawn to a point $B$ on the circle, and angle $ABO = 31^\circ$.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=2.4]
    \coordinate (O) at (0,0);
    \draw[name path=circ] (0,0) circle (1);
    \fill[black] (O) circle (1pt);
    \node[below right] at (O) {$O$};

    % A at angle 0 (rightmost point of circle) so tangent is vertical
    \coordinate (A) at (0:1);
    \node[right] at (A) {$A$};

    % B placed so that angle ABO = 31 degrees (isosceles triangle OAB, OA=OB=radius)
    % triangle OAB isosceles with OA=OB, base angle at A and B equal = 31 deg, so angle AOB = 180-62=118
    \coordinate (B) at (118:1);
    \node[above left] at (B) {$B$};

    \draw (O) -- (A);
    \draw (O) -- (B);
    \draw (A) -- (B);

    % Tangent line at A, vertical line through A since A is at angle 0
    \draw (A) -- ++(0,1.4) coordinate (T);
    \draw (A) -- ++(0,-0.6) coordinate (Tlow);
    \node[above] at (T) {$T$};

    % mark right angle between OA and tangent
    \tkzMarkRightAngle[size=.25](O,A,T);

    % mark angle ABO = 31 degrees
    \pic [draw, "$31^\circ$", angle eccentricity=1.5, angle radius=0.4cm] {angle = O--B--A};

    % mark equal radii
    \tkzMarkSegment[pos=.5,mark=|](O,A);
    \tkzMarkSegment[pos=.5,mark=|](O,B);

\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\noindent Given that angle $ABO = 31^\circ$, work out the size of angle $OAT$, and hence find the size of angle $BAT$.
